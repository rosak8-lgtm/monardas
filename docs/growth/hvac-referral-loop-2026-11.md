## Growth loop
Share a free public self-check at /hvac/scorecard -> HVAC contractor notices a self-reported process gap -> optional free backlog assessment -> individually scoped, paid managed pilot -> record real measurable outcome -> ask happy customer for a referral. Independently, an HVAC marketing agency can share the tool with its contractor clients; no referral payment or integration is claimed until agreed.

## Scope safeguards
The website already has an illustrative revenue calculator and fictional sample demo. This scorecard is different: it checks five controls and NEVER claims to predict recovered revenue. No homeowner data is requested, stored or transmitted, and no emails or customer outreach are automated by the tool. Any homeowner follow-up requires the contractor's confirmation of eligible contacts, exclusions, channels and message copy. Do not claim exclusive differentiation: ServiceTitan offers its own automated recovery of unsold estimates (2026).

## 14-day validation and measurement
1. Check local build, mobile keyboard accessibility and link behavior. Deploy only after passing QA.
2. Individually verify 15-25 genuinely relevant agencies / consultants; exclude previous sends across OCI, Gmail and Resend, respect opt-outs and provider limits.
3. Test up to 10 personalized official partner introductions with no generic blast or fabricated permission.
4. Measure scorecard visits, free-assessment submissions, real replies, demos, signed pilots, and collected revenue. Targets: 2 demos and 1 paid pilot; these are not forecasts.
5. If no meaningful conversation after 10-15 qualified approaches, change message/segment before increasing send volume.

No external messages, paid ads, CRM writes or Oracle queue changes are part of this development change.
