# MONARDAS · 30-day founding contractor pilot · operating brief
Date: 2026-11-14. **Offer**, not executed contract. No founding customers signed yet.

## Why 30 days
In Nov 2026 public founding-beta examples include Recuptive's 30-day free real-lead beta (recuptive.com/beta) and Biptach's 60-day free HVAC software pilot (biptach.com/founding-pilot). For a founder-led **service**, 30 days and capped work are a better first validation cycle: a week to prepare, 2–3 weeks for follow-up activity, a final business review, and then an optional paid continuation decision. Do not extrapolate their results or claim customer proof.

## What selected HVAC contractor receives — $0
- Limited to the first **two** accepted independent US residential HVAC businesses; not first-come guaranteed.
- **30 consecutive calendar days** from the mutually agreed activation date, not from the website application.
- Exactly **one** controlled batch of **up to 25** eligible unsold AC, heat pump or furnace replacement quotes. No adding unlimited new quotes mid-pilot.
- **Up to two** separately approved follow-up actions per eligible quote; the action includes preparing a contractor-approved message, assigning an owner, logging or reviewing an outcome, and/or safely executing the outreach if explicitly authorized. Not 25 daily campaigns.
- Owner kickoff (15–30 min), operational review of minimized records, prioritized worklist, contractor-approved message drafts, weekly status reviews and final report.
- Distinguish: inspected quote, eligible quote, excluded quote, drafted message, actual message sent, actual recipient reply, qualified conversation, appointment, job closed (client confirmed), revenue (client evidenced). Zeroes are acceptable.
- No mandatory card, no deposit, no automatic rollover, no subscription. Future managed service is a separate written agreement.

## Conditions before homeowner outreach
- Do not ask for homeowner names, addresses, phone numbers, detailed free-text messages, account credentials, or raw CSVs via the public application form.
- Scope and data-handling authorization signed before any limited personally identifying data is processed. Use a secure authorized transfer channel rather than ordinary public forms.
- Verify **source, current status, eligibility, contact permissions, permitted channel, opt-outs/suppression, timing and duplicates** for every record. A contractor's historical estimate does not inherently grant permission for automated marketing calls or texts.
- Contractor controls customer relationship, pricing, financing, technical advice and close.
- Contractor sends using its lawful channels by default. Any MONARDAS-owned customer outreach requires additional written authorization, approved text, sender identity/channel, stop conditions and technically verified safeguards. If such compliance or infrastructure is not available, operate in draft/assist mode only.
- Never guarantee replies, bookings, sales or attributed recovered revenue; no artificial demo data represented as customer outcomes.
- Feedback and identifiable public case studies only with separate explicit consent. No obligation to provide a testimonial.

## Fit screen (first 15-minute call)
- Independent US residential HVAC company, authorized decision maker participates.
- At least 10 relevant open/unsold replacement quotes in an identifiable recent pipeline; up to 25 sampled.
- Salesperson or owner ready to approve follow-up tasks and record real outcomes.
- Can provide de-identified fields for initial audit; agrees on privacy and contact guardrails.
- Not currently under a better-functioning follow-up process; owner agrees to weekly check-ins.
- Decline if no owner, no suitable quotes, no ability to exclude ineligible contact, or expectation that MONARDAS promises guaranteed revenue.

## Conversion milestone
At Day 21, if meaningful progress exists, ask for a 15-minute paid-option discussion; do **not** interrupt the remaining free service. At Day 30, deliver the report and allow the shop to stop with $0 due. A paid ongoing pilot can be proposed at a transparent individually agreed rate; $300–$500 is a hypothesis from earlier discussions, not an agreed or published price. The first paid contract and funds received are the proof of commercial viability.
